# CareerGraph recording script (3–5 minutes)

1. Open Dashboard. Introduce CareerGraph and Maya’s role-match percentage, gaps, and project signal.
2. Open Roles, choose Full Stack Developer, and show skills, the relationship graph, and project coverage.
3. Open Skills, select React, and point out prerequisite and multi-hop trails.
4. Open Career path, choose JavaScript → Express.js, click **Map my path**, and explain the returned shortest path.
5. Open My career, switch profiles, and show owned skills, ranked readiness, and roles found within three prerequisite hops.
6. Open Courses, add a course, select the skills it teaches, and show the new course-to-skill links in the catalog.
7. Close with why CognoDB matters: roles, skills, projects, courses, resources, and users are connected; queries use traversal rather than recursive joins.

Optional engineering close: show `.env.example`, `scripts/seed.js`, and one parameterized Cypher module.
